Wire Anglerfish

November 2022

An anglerfish sculpture made from recycled steel and copper wire, with a glowing LED lure.

Materials: Recycled steel and copper wire, LED Light

Dimensions: 11 H × 19 W × 10 D cm

I've always enjoyed working with wire, in fact, whenever I finish using a notebook, I make sure to carefully remove the wire spine for potential use in other projects. This was the perfect opportunity to use this highly versatile material.

Wire makes it much easier to design intricate shapes, and it's quite forgiving. Additionally, its metallic appearance produces an industrial quality that can be combined with other materials to achieve a more lifelike look.

Animals capable of generating their own light have always fascinated me, and the anglerfish was the ideal candidate for the sculpture I had envisioned. I must admit, I really wanted to incorporate LEDs into this project as well.

The green light creates a strong contrast with the dark background, producing a mysterious, almost alien-like effect, which is precisely what I was aiming for.

It took me several hours to craft this piece, beginning with the frame and then carefully adding thinner-gauge wire to create the "skin." It was all worth it. This is one of my favorite sculptures.
